Hair care.

Keeping it performing beautifully.

Product Selection Hair Care Performance Orders

What products should be used?

Use professional-quality, moisturizing products formulated for color-treated or extension hair.

A gentle shampoo, moisturizing conditioner, lightweight leave-in product, and heat protectant are typically all that is needed. Avoid excessive product buildup and keep heavy conditioners and oils away from attachment points.

Your Purple Crown professional can recommend a routine for the client’s specific hair and installation.

How should the hair be washed and conditioned?

Detangle the hair before washing. Cleanse gently in a downward motion using lukewarm water, without twisting or piling the hair.

Apply conditioner through the mid-lengths and ends and rinse thoroughly. Avoid placing conditioner directly on attachment points unless instructed by the stylist.

How should the hair be brushed and dried?

Support the hair near the attachment area and begin brushing at the ends, gradually working upward.

After washing, dry the scalp, rows, attachment points, and weft foundation completely. Never sleep with wet or damp extensions.

Because our finished textures are professionally permed, light styling may be needed after washing to restore the preferred wave or curl pattern.

Can heat tools be used?

Yes. Apply an extension-safe heat protectant and do not exceed 350°F.

Use the lowest effective temperature and avoid applying direct heat to beads, thread, lace, or other attachment points.

What should clients avoid?

For the best experience, avoid:

  • Sleeping with wet hair
  • Aggressive brushing
  • Excessive heat
  • Heavy product buildup
  • Applying oils or conditioner to attachment points
  • Delaying maintenance appointments
  • Leaving chlorine, salt water, or sunscreen in the hair
  • Attempting to remove or reposition extensions at home

A loose braid or low ponytail is recommended during sleep and physical activity.

How often is professional maintenance required?

Maintenance is generally required every four to eight weeks, depending on the installation method, natural growth, placement, lifestyle, and home care.

The stylist will establish the appropriate schedule during the consultation and adjust it as needed.
