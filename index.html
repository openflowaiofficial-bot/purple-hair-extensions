Our Solutions

Every order is built to your specification — length, density, texture, and color blended to the strand. Choose the system that fits your client.

Mesh Integration

A breathable, ultra-fine mesh system integrated with the client's natural hair — ideal for fine, thinning, or fragile hair. No tension, no adhesive, full scalp comfort with a seamless, undetectable finish.

  • Custom-built to head measurements
  • Ideal for volume loss & thinning hair
  • 6–8 week maintenance cycle
  • Certification training included
≋

Premium Wefts

Hand-tied and machine wefts in luxurious double-drawn Remy hair. Flexible installation — beaded rows, sew-in, or hybrid methods — with flat, lightweight seams that lie flush to the head.

  • Hand-tied & machine weft options
  • 14"–26" lengths, double-drawn density
  • Custom color blending & rooted shades
  • Reusable up to 12 months
