The Purple Crown standard

Quality you can see, feel, and trust behind the chair.

Our extension collections are crafted from premium Indian Temple hair selected for its natural movement, dimensional color, beautiful texture, and professional performance.

The cuticles remain aligned in one direction, helping the strands move together naturally and reducing unnecessary friction and tangling. The hair is not silicone-coated to create a temporary artificial finish. What you experience is the natural character and quality of the hair itself.

Every collection is chosen with the professional in mind — from the initial consultation and color match to installation, maintenance, removal, and reinstallation.

Why direction matters

Aligned from root to end.

Cuticle alignment matters. When the cuticles run in the same direction, the strands move together more naturally and experience less friction.

The collections

Three lines, made for the chair.

  1. Plus Lace Wefts

    Designed with a fine, flexible lace foundation that creates a lightweight, low-profile finish. Plus Lace Wefts allow for comfortable, discreet placement and beautiful movement while giving the stylist flexibility to customize the installation.

  2. Machine Wefts

    Professionally constructed for strength, density, and dependable installation. Machine Wefts offer flexible placement and the fullness needed to create customized length, volume, and dimension.

  3. Professional Tools & Supplies

    A curated selection of beads, thread, needles, and professional essentials used within The Purple Crown system. Chosen to help stylists work efficiently and maintain consistency throughout the service.

Education first

Crown Your Style.

Crown Your Style is a hands-on extension education experience created for licensed professionals who want to strengthen their technical skill, elevate the client experience, and build a more confident extension business.

Stylists work with The Purple Crown system while learning how to approach the entire service — from consultation and installation planning through maintenance and long-term client care.

After successfully completing the class, stylists may be invited to create a professional account. Wholesale access is activated only after account review and approval.

Professional access

Two ways in.

Professional accounts are not open to the general public. There are two pathways, and both end with an account reviewed and approved by The Purple Crown.

Licensed professionals may complete Crown Your Style and then receive an invitation to create a professional account. Accounts are reviewed before wholesale access is activated. This is the primary pathway for individual professionals.
