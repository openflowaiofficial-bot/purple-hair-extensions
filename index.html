Two systems

One is for adding hair.
One is for replacing what's gone.

Most houses sell you wefts and call it a catalogue. A client with real thinning cannot carry a weft — the tension goes straight into fragile hair. So we build both, and we will tell you which one your client actually needs.

Mesh integration — crown placement, week six
Method 01

Mesh Integration

A breathable mesh is fitted over the thinning area. Your client's own hair is pulled through it, and hair is hand-tied to the mesh — not to her hair. No adhesive, no tension on fragile strands, no shaving.

  • For moderate to advanced thinning and early loss
  • Built to head measurements you take in the chair
  • HD lace shades matched to the client's scalp
  • 6–8 week maintenance cycle

Hand-tied weft — beaded row, seam flush to the head
Method 02

Hand-Tied & Machine Wefts

Double-drawn Remy in flat, lightweight rows. Hand-tied where the seam has to disappear, machine-tied where the client needs density and a weft she can cut. Install beaded, sewn, or hybrid.

  • For clients with healthy hair who want length or volume
  • 14″–26″, double-drawn from root to tip
  • Rooted and custom-blended shades, cut to order
  • Reusable up to twelve months with correct maintenance

Color lab — matching a client cutting against stock
The color lab

Send a cutting. We match it and send a sample back.

Forty shades in stock covers most of the chair. It does not cover the client who walked in with a color you spent three appointments building.

For those, mail us a cutting or a photo in daylight. Our colorists formulate, dye, and return a physical sample for your approval before the full order runs. Rooted melts, dimensional blends, and babylights are formulated the same way — as a shade, not as an add-on.
