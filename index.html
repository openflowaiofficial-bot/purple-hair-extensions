The hair

Forty years of access, in one place.

We work first-hand in Texas with a supplier who has served the professional studio and beauty industry in Hollywood for more than forty years.

The hair is premium Indian Temple hair — single-direction, one donor — with the cuticle running the way it grew. It is never silicone-coated. What you hold on the first day is what your client still has months later, because nothing was added to make it feel that way in the box.

That access is the reason this house exists. Everything else we do is built to make sure the hair reaches a professional who knows what to do with it.
