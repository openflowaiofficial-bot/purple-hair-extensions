Who we are

Created for the professional behind the transformation.

The Purple Crown Extensions was built around a simple belief: exceptional hair produces its best results when it is supported by exceptional education.

We serve licensed beauty professionals and salon owners who care about the complete extension experience — quality hair, thoughtful consultations, precise installation, beautiful customization, responsible maintenance, and lasting professional relationships.

Our collections feature premium Indian Temple hair selected for natural movement, dimensional color, and professional performance. We pair that hair with hands-on education and continued support so members of The Crown can work with greater knowledge, consistency, and confidence.

Each ponytail comes from one donor and is hand-pulled — collected, sorted, aligned, and prepared entirely by hand, with its original root-to-tip direction preserved. The cuticle layer remains intact and aligned, and the hair is not silicone-coated to create a temporary artificial finish.

Education first

Hair is only half of the result.

Extension results depend on the whole service — consultation, hair selection, installation, customization, maintenance, and home care.

So education comes first. Crown Your Style guides licensed professionals through the full client journey on The Purple Crown system, from planning an installation to maintaining it and caring for the client long afterwards.

It is also why professional accounts are not open to the general public. Every account is reviewed and approved individually before wholesale access is activated.

Our commitment

To the professional, first and last.

  1. Professional access only

    There is no consumer storefront and no retail line. Professional accounts are not open to the general public.

  2. Pricing stays behind the login

    Wholesale pricing is never shown publicly, so the value of your service is never undercut by a number a client can find.

  3. Every account is reviewed

    Creating an account does not automatically grant wholesale access. Each professional is reviewed and approved individually.

  4. The hair is described accurately

    Single-donor, hand-pulled, cuticle-aligned, professionally processed Remy Indian Temple hair. The finished wave and curl patterns are professionally permed, and we say so.
