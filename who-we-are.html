Who we are

A house built around the hair itself.

The Purple Crown exists because of a supply relationship most brands cannot get, and a conviction that hair this good belongs only in trained hands.

Most extension brands begin with a logo and then go looking for hair. We began the other way round.

Our access is in Texas, first-hand, to a supplier who has been providing hair to the professional studio and beauty industry in Hollywood for more than forty years. That is a working relationship, not a reseller arrangement — and it is the single reason the quality is consistent from one order to the next.

The hair is premium Indian Temple hair. It is single-direction, one donor, with every cuticle running the way it grew. It is not silicone-coated — not lightly, not ever. Coating is how hair is made to feel expensive in the box and ordinary by the third wash, and we would rather you judged ours in month nine.

We make no claims beyond that, because we do not need to. Order it, work with it, and let it settle the argument.

Education first

Hair is only half of the result.

The finest hair in the world, placed badly, is a disappointed client and a stylist who gets the blame.

So we teach before we sell. Crown Your Style certification covers placement, tension, sectioning and blending — on the exact hair you will be ordering, not on a demonstration substitute.

It is also why we do not open wholesale to anyone who asks. Every account is approved by hand, because our name goes on the head as surely as yours does.

Our commitment

To the professional, first and last.

  1. We do not sell to the public

    There is no consumer storefront and no retail line. Your client cannot buy this hair anywhere except from you.

  2. Pricing stays behind the login

    Wholesale pricing is never shown publicly, so the value of your service is never undercut by a number a client can find.

  3. Every account is approved by hand

    Creating an account does not grant access. Each professional is reviewed and approved individually.

  4. The hair is exactly what we say it is

    Single-direction, one donor, never silicone-coated. If we cannot stand behind a lot, we do not sell it.
