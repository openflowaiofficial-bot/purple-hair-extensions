Certification is a condition of the account, not an upsell. Both methods, hands-on or virtual, on a mannequin and then a model — because a system installed badly is our name on her head as much as yours. We teach licensed professionals only: the one way to buy this hair is to sit in a partner's chair.

The certifications

Two methods. Certify on one or both.

Cert 01

Mesh Integration

The harder of the two and the one with almost no competition in most markets. Consultation, mapping, measurement, base fitting, drawing the bio hair through, blending, and the maintenance cycle.

  • Format2 days hands-on, or 3 virtual sessions
  • PracticalMannequin, then a live model you bring
  • PrerequisiteActive cosmetology license
  • CostIncluded with an approved account
Cert 02

Hand-Tied & Machine Wefts

Row placement, bead sizing, tension control, sectioning for a client's density, cutting into the row, and the move-up. Includes the blending work most stylists were never taught.

  • Format1 day hands-on, or 2 virtual sessions
  • PracticalMannequin, then a live model you bring
  • PrerequisiteActive cosmetology license
  • CostIncluded with an approved account
Beyond the install

Installing is the easy half.

Most stylists who stall on extensions can install fine. What they cannot do yet is price the service, hold the consultation, and keep the client coming back.

So certification covers the business of the service as well as the technique. You leave with a price built on your own chair rate, a consultation script, and a maintenance calendar you can actually hold a client to.

  • Pricing the service against your chair rate, not a competitor's menu
  • Running the consultation, including telling a client no
  • Photographing your own work so it sells the next appointment
  • Setting and holding the maintenance cycle
  • Aftercare the client will actually follow
  • Reading when a client needs mesh rather than a weft
  • Handling a color correction on installed hair
  • What to do when an install goes wrong
