What you can specify

Six axes. Set every one of them.

An order form here is not a size chart. You are writing a build sheet, and any combination below is a valid order. The lab is open to certified partners only — there is no consumer storefront and no way for your client to order around you.

COLOR — LEVEL & TONE

Color

Stock shade, rooted melt, dimensional blend, babylights, or matched to a cutting you mail us. Tell us the level and tone you want to end on and we formulate to it.

LENGTH — INCHES

Length

14″ to 26″ on wefts, 10″ to 24″ on mesh systems. Cut to a graduated length across the row if you want the layers built in before you install.

DENSITY — GRAMS PER ROW

Density

Weight per row on wefts. Graduated tie density on mesh — lighter at the perimeter, heavier through the crown, set per order to the hair she has left.

TEXTURE — PATTERN

Texture

Straight, soft wave, blowout, or a curl pattern matched to her natural texture so the blend holds after a wash rather than only after a style.

WIDTH & BASE — INCHES

Width & base

Hand-tied rows sized in 1″ increments. Mesh bases cut to the four measurements you take in the chair, with the lace tone matched to her scalp.

FINISH — GLOSS / UNCOATED

Finish

Uncoated as standard. Ask for a gloss finish if a client wants it, and we will tell you honestly how long it lasts.

How a match runs

You approve the sample. Then we run the order.

No custom order goes to production off a photograph alone. You hold the sample against her hair first.

  1. Send the cutting

    You — day one

    A cutting from the nape is best. If she will not part with one, three photos in north-facing daylight with the shade ring held against her mid-lengths.

  2. Formulation

    Our colorists — 2 to 3 days

    We read the cutting under standardized light, pick the closest base, and write a formula. Rooted work is formulated as two shades with a melt zone, not dipped.

  3. Sample to your salon

    Post — 2 to 4 days

    A physical sample in the formulated shade, cut from the same lot the order will run on. Hold it against her hair, wet and dry, in your own salon light.

  4. Approve, then production

    Our lab — 10 to 14 days

    You approve or you send corrections. Corrections are free, twice. The formula is filed against her name so a reorder next year is the same hair.

The formula archive

A match is filed under her name, not the order number.

The reason clients leave a stylist over extensions is rarely the first install. It is the reorder that came back a shade off.

Every custom formula we mix is filed against the client, the lot, and your salon. When she comes back in fourteen months, we run the same formula on the same source — so the new row disappears into the old one.

The archive belongs to your salon. If a stylist leaves, the file stays with you.
