Crown Your Style

Become a certified stylist.

Certification is the primary path into The Purple Crown. Complete a class and you are invited to open a professional account with wholesale access.

The class

A day on the hair you will actually order.

Crown Your Style is taught on Purple Crown hair, not on a demonstration substitute, so what you practise is what you will place in your own chair.

You work through placement and sectioning, tension and row spacing, blending into the client’s own hair, and the maintenance cycle that keeps an install looking right months later.

Classes are small on purpose. You leave certified, confident, and invited to open a professional account.

The path

From class to wholesale access.

  1. Choose a class

    Pick a date and location that suits you from the calendar below.

  2. Register and pay

    Your seat is confirmed by email as soon as payment clears.

  3. Attend and certify

    A full day of hands-on training, taught on Purple Crown hair.

  4. Receive your invitation

    After certifying you are invited to create your professional account.

  5. Approval and access

    We approve your account by hand, and wholesale pricing opens. Creating an account alone does not grant access.
