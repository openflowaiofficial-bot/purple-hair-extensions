Crown Your Style

Crown Your Style Masterclass.

Crown Your Style is a hands-on extension education experience created for licensed professionals who want to strengthen their technical skill, elevate the client experience, and build a more confident extension business.

The class

Hands-on education designed for the realities of working behind the chair.

You will learn more than how to complete an installation. Crown Your Style guides you through the full client journey: consultation, hair selection, placement planning, installation, blending, maintenance, pricing, rebooking, and long-term client care.

Stylists work with The Purple Crown system while learning how to approach the entire service — from consultation and installation planning through maintenance and long-term client care.

Class sizes are intentionally limited to support focused instruction and hands-on learning.

The path

From class to wholesale access.

  1. Choose a class

    Select an available class from the schedule below.

  2. Register and pay

    Your seat is reserved once payment is received.

  3. Attend and certify

    Two days of hands-on training on The Purple Crown system.

  4. Receive your invitation

    After successfully completing the class, stylists may be invited to create a professional account.

  5. Review and approval

    Wholesale access is activated only after account review and approval. Completing certification does not automatically activate wholesale access.

The curriculum

What you’ll learn.

  • Extension fundamentals and product knowledge
  • Client consultations and expectation setting
  • Hair and color selection
  • Determining the appropriate amount of hair
  • Sectioning and row placement
  • Bead placement and secure foundations
  • Proper tension and scalp safety
  • Installation technique
  • Customization, cutting, and blending
  • Creating natural-looking length, fullness, and dimension
  • Maintenance schedules and client home care
  • Removal and reinstallation
  • Troubleshooting common installation concerns
  • Service pricing and profitability
  • Rebooking and client retention
  • Building confidence throughout the consultation and service

Completing Crown Your Style is more than learning an installation method. It is the beginning of your relationship with The Purple Crown professional community.
