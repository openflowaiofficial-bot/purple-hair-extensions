When will my order ship?

In-stock products normally leave The Purple Crown within two to three business days.

Custom orders are individually quoted and may require several months to complete. Tracking information is provided when an order ships.

Can an item be returned or exchanged?

Eligible unopened and unaltered products may be submitted for return or exchange consideration within 14 days of delivery.

Return authorization is required, and customers are responsible for return shipping. A 25% restocking fee may apply.

Tools, promotional products, sale items, custom orders, and products that have been opened, installed, washed, cut, colored, or chemically altered are final sale.

The Purple Crown does not provide cash refunds except where required by law. Approved resolutions may include an exchange, replacement, or account credit.

What if my order arrives incorrect or damaged?

Inspect your order as soon as it arrives.

Contact support@purplecrownextensions.com within three business days if an item is missing, incorrect, or visibly damaged. Please keep the packaging and include photographs so we can review the order promptly.

Where can I read the complete policy?

Please review our complete Shipping, Returns, and Product Concern Policy before ordering or performing services on the hair.
