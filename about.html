Who we are

The House

A small trade house that builds custom extension systems for licensed professionals. We have no retail storefront, no clip-in line, and no plans for either.

Our standard

Built for the stylist who gets blamed.

When extensions fail, the client does not blame the manufacturer. She blames the person who put them in.

That is the whole reason this house exists. We are a trade supplier, which means our actual customer is a stylist standing behind a chair with her reputation in her hands. Every decision we make is downstream of that.

We are also deliberately small. We turn away applications in areas where we are already saturated, because a partner who cannot fill a book is a partner who leaves. If you apply and we say no, that is why — and we will tell you plainly rather than take the order.

  • Double-drawn Because tapering ends look like your bad install
  • Cuticle intact Because dead hair in month three is your phone ringing
  • Certification required Because a system fitted wrong is your review

Sourcing

Traceable, or we do not buy it.

Hair is an agricultural product with a supply chain most suppliers would rather you did not ask about. Ask us.

We buy ethically sourced hair with documented origin, single-donor where the order allows it. Cuticles arrive intact and aligned — never acid-bathed, never silicone-coated to fake a gloss that washes out. Blondes are toned to level in our own lab rather than bleached on arrival, so the hair still takes color when you need to adjust it.

If you ask your account manager where a specific lot came from, you will get an answer. If we cannot answer, we do not sell that lot.

What we commit to

Six promises, and what breaks them.

Each of these is a commitment your account manager can be held to. If we break one, say so and we will make it right.

EXCLUSIVITY

We never sell to the public

No consumer storefront, no marketplace listings, no direct-to-client channel. The only way to get this hair is through a partner's chair.

PRICING

One price sheet

Trade pricing is published to every partner at the same rate. We do not run public promotions that undercut the price you paid.

TERRITORY

Territory honesty

We tell you if your area is already served before you invest in certification, rather than after.

ARCHIVE

Your formula archive is yours

Client color files stay with your salon. If a stylist moves on, the file does not go with them.

COMMITMENT

No lock-in

No minimum order to apply, no monthly commitment, no contract term. Stop ordering whenever you like and the account stays open.

CANDOUR

We will say no

To an order that will not work, a client who is not a candidate, and a territory we cannot serve properly. A supplier who never says no is selling.
