Is some shedding normal?

A small amount of shedding may occur during normal wear, brushing, washing, installation, and maintenance.

If shedding becomes sudden, excessive, or concentrated in one area, pause additional alteration and contact The Purple Crown for guidance.

What can contribute to tangling or matting?

Common causes include sleeping with wet hair, incomplete drying, product buildup, improper brushing, friction, overdue maintenance, environmental exposure, excessive heat, or chemical overprocessing.

Consistent home care and timely professional maintenance are essential to keeping the hair performing beautifully.

What should I do if I have a concern?

Pause further coloring, cutting, installation, or alteration and contact us promptly.

Email support@purplecrownextensions.com with your order number, a brief description, and clear photographs or video. Our team will let you know if additional information is needed.

How are product concerns handled?

Every concern is reviewed individually.

Depending on the circumstances, The Purple Crown may offer an exchange, replacement, or account credit. Please retain the hair, packaging, and order information until the review is complete.

Post-installation concerns should be reported within 30 days of installation.
