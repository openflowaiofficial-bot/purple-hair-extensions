### What makes The Purple Crown Extensions different?

Our extensions are crafted from premium Indian Temple hair selected for its softness, strength, movement, and professional performance.

The cuticles remain aligned in one direction, helping the strands move together smoothly and reducing unnecessary friction. The hair is not silicone-coated to create a temporary artificial finish.

Select textures are professionally permed to create their finished wave or curl pattern. We pair the hair with hands-on education so Purple Crown professionals understand how to install, customize, color, maintain, and care for each collection properly.

### Is the texture natural?

The wave or curl pattern is professionally permed and is not the hair's original, naturally occurring texture.

Perming allows us to offer more consistent texture options throughout our collections. The texture should be treated as professionally processed hair and cared for according to The Purple Crown's recommendations.

### Will the texture remain exactly the same over time?

Because the texture is professionally permed, it may relax or change gradually with washing, heat styling, chemical services, environmental exposure, product buildup, and repeated wear.

Following the recommended cleansing, conditioning, drying, and styling routine will help maintain the intended pattern. The hair may need to be styled after washing to recreate the client's preferred finish.

### Can the hair be colored?

Yes. Purple Crown hair may be professionally colored.

Because extension hair can process differently from the client's natural hair, a strand test is always recommended before applying color to the complete set. Coloring should be performed by a licensed professional experienced with extension hair.

The stylist should evaluate the hair's existing color, texture, processing history, porosity, and condition before selecting a formula.

### Can the hair be lightened?

Yes. Purple Crown hair may be professionally lightened.

A strand test is always recommended before lightening the complete set. The strand test allows the stylist to evaluate the lift, underlying pigment, integrity, texture response, and expected processing time.

Lightening should be performed by a licensed professional experienced with extension hair. The stylist should use professional judgment and avoid pushing the hair beyond the condition demonstrated by the strand test.

### Can the hair be toned?

Yes. Purple Crown hair may be professionally toned or color-deposited.

A strand test is always recommended because extension hair and the client's natural hair may have different porosity and may process at different speeds. The stylist should verify the formula, timing, undertone, and final result before applying it to the entire set.

### Will coloring or lightening affect the texture?

It can. Coloring, lightening, toning, repeated heat styling, and other chemical services may affect the condition or permed texture of the hair.

A strand test should be used to evaluate both the color result and the way the texture responds before proceeding with the complete set. After chemical services, the hair may require additional conditioning and styling to maintain the desired pattern.

### Will coloring or lightening affect returns, exchanges, or product-concern eligibility?

Hair that has been colored, lightened, toned, chemically altered, cut, installed, washed, or otherwise modified may be subject to different return, exchange, or product-review conditions.

Review The Purple Crown's current policies before altering the hair. A successful strand test does not replace professional judgment or guarantee that chemically processed hair will qualify for return or exchange.

# Understanding Our Hair

Second sheet supplied by the owner 2026-08-28. Supersedes anything above where
they differ.

**Approved descriptor — use this wording:**
> Single-donor, hand-pulled, cuticle-aligned, professionally processed Remy
> Indian Temple hair.

**Never describe the hair as** virgin, raw, unprocessed, or naturally textured.
The wave and curl patterns are professionally permed.

### What does "hand-pulled" mean?

Hand-pulled means our hair is collected, sorted, aligned, and prepared entirely by hand.

The hair's original root-to-tip direction is preserved throughout the process. No mechanical agitation or automated alignment equipment is used.

This careful process helps maintain the integrity of the strands and ensures that every cuticle continues to face the same direction.

### Is Purple Crown hair machine-agitated?

No. Purple Crown hair is hand-pulled and is never machine-agitated.

Machine agitation may be used by some manufacturers to organize or align loose, mixed-direction hair mechanically. Our hair does not go through that process.

Each ponytail is handled and prepared by hand while preserving its original root-to-tip direction.

### Is the hair collected from one donor?

Yes. Each ponytail comes from one donor.

The hair is not created by combining loose hair from multiple donors into one mixed bundle. Keeping each ponytail together helps preserve consistency, cuticle direction, and the original integrity of the hair.

### Is loose, brushed, fallen, or floor-collected hair included?

No.

Our hair is not collected from brushes, salon floors, or loose mixed-hair sources. The hair remains together as a single-donor ponytail so its original root-to-tip direction can be maintained.

### Are the cuticles intact?

Yes. The hair retains its cuticle layer.

The cuticle is the protective outer layer of each strand. Maintaining the cuticle helps preserve the hair's integrity, movement, softness, and professional performance.

### Are all the cuticles aligned in the same direction?

Yes. Every strand is maintained in its original root-to-tip direction.

When the cuticles remain aligned, the strands move together more smoothly and experience less unnecessary friction than mixed-direction hair.

### Is Purple Crown hair Remy?

Yes. Purple Crown hair may accurately be described as processed Remy hair.

"Remy" means the cuticles remain intact and all strands are aligned in the same root-to-tip direction. Hair can still be considered Remy after professional color or texture processing as long as its cuticle structure and directional alignment are preserved.

Because our finished textures are professionally permed, the hair should not be described as virgin, raw, unprocessed, or naturally textured.

The appropriate description is: Single-donor, hand-pulled, cuticle-aligned, professionally processed Remy Indian Temple hair.

### Is the wave or curl pattern natural?

No. The finished wave or curl pattern is professionally permed to create the intended texture.

Although the texture is not the hair's naturally occurring pattern, the hair itself remains genuine human Indian Temple hair with intact, aligned cuticles.

Professional perming allows us to create more consistent texture options across our collections.

### Is the hair silicone-coated?

No. Purple Crown hair does not contain silicone coatings or temporary surface coatings designed to create an artificial finish.

The hair's softness, movement, and appearance come from the quality and condition of the hair itself—not from a coating that is intended to wash away.

### Can Purple Crown hair be colored?

Yes. Purple Crown hair is safe for professional coloring.

Because extension hair may process differently from the client's natural hair, a strand test is always recommended before applying color to the complete set.

Color services should be performed by a licensed professional experienced with extension hair.

### Can Purple Crown hair be lightened?

Yes. Purple Crown hair is safe for professional lightening.

A strand test is always recommended before lightening the complete set. The strand test allows the stylist to evaluate: the degree of lift; underlying pigment; processing time; the condition of the hair; the response of the permed texture; the expected final result.

The stylist should use professional judgment and should not process the complete set beyond what the test strand demonstrates the hair can safely support.

### Can the hair be toned?

Yes. Purple Crown hair may be professionally toned or color-deposited.

A strand test is always recommended because extension hair and the client's natural hair may have different porosity and may process at different speeds.

### Can chemical services affect the permed texture?

Yes. Coloring, lightening, toning, and other chemical services can affect the condition or professionally permed texture of the hair.

The strand test should be evaluated for both the color result and the texture response before the complete set is processed. Chemically serviced hair may require additional conditioning and styling to maintain the desired pattern.

### How should we describe the Purple Crown standard?

Purple Crown hair is: premium Indian Temple hair; collected as a ponytail; sourced from one donor; hand-pulled and prepared entirely by hand; never machine-agitated; free from loose, brushed, fallen, and floor-collected hair; cuticle intact; aligned from root to tip; processed Remy hair; professionally permed to create the finished texture; safe for professional coloring and lightening; always recommended for strand testing before chemical services; free from silicone and temporary surface coatings.
