Method 01

Mesh
Integration

For the client who cannot carry a weft. The hair attaches to a breathable mesh instead of to her own strands, so nothing pulls, nothing is shaved, and what she still has keeps growing underneath.

Who it is for

Between extensions and a wig, there was nothing. This is that.

A client with moderate thinning is the hardest appointment in the book. Extensions put weight and tension straight into fragile hair and make the loss worse. A wig means giving up what she still has.

Mesh integration sits between the two. A fine, hypoallergenic mesh is fitted over the thinning area. Her own hair is drawn up through the mesh and left free. Our hair is hand-tied to the mesh itself, never to her own strands. The result carries no load on her hair, and her scalp still breathes.

Read the indications honestly before you sell it — this system is a poor fit for some clients, and the list below is the one we use ourselves.

Indicated

Recommend it for

  • Moderate to advanced diffuse thinning
  • Early-stage androgenetic loss with hair still present
  • Postpartum and post-illness shedding
  • Traction damage from years of tension methods
  • Fine or fragile hair that cannot hold a bead row
  • Clients who want density without a daily routine
Not indicated

Send it back for

  • Total loss with no anchor hair — fit a wig instead
  • Active scalp disease or untreated inflammation
  • Clients unwilling to return every 6–8 weeks
  • Anyone chasing length alone on healthy hair — sell a weft
How the system is built

Measured in your chair. Tied in our lab.

Nothing about a mesh system is stock. The base is cut to her head, the density is set to her remaining hair, and the lace is matched to her scalp.

  1. Consultation & measurement

    In your chair — 45 minutes

    You map the thinning area, take four measurements, photograph the scalp in daylight, and take a cutting from the nape. We send you the measurement card in your welcome kit.

  2. Base cut & lace match

    Our lab — 3 to 5 days

    The mesh base is cut to your map and the HD lace shade is chosen against her scalp photo. Six lace tones cover most complexions; we will dye one if none of them disappear.

  3. Hand-tying & color

    Our lab — 10 to 14 days

    Hair is tied to the mesh at graduated density — lighter at the perimeter so the blend has no edge, heavier through the crown where she wants coverage. Color is formulated against the cutting you sent.

  4. Fitting & integration

    In your chair — 2 to 3 hours

    You attach the base, draw her hair through the mesh, then cut and blend the system into her own hair. She walks out finished, not staged for a second visit.
